## New Features
- when a map has whitelist faction list, and there are none of those factions in the current level's faction pool, just randomize using the map's whitelist instead [c183abc]
- when limited time quests run out of time, they now have the option to skip their state to rewarded, instead of being able to accept it again (any quests that require this quest can then be accepted as if it was successfully completed, you just wont be able to get the rewards from that timed out quest) [7325cdc]
- quest tasks now stay visually completed when their quests are now in the completed quest list (pre-rewarded) (also, on the previous commit, the 2 quests that use the expire day are no retry) [0c2c1e9]
