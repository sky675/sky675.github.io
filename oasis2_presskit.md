As part of an up-and-coming outfit of mercenaries, break your way into a shady underworld ruled by mega-corporations. Make yourself known through contracts from corporations, criminal organizations, and anyone else who's willing to pay.

## Description

OASIS II is a 1-5 player co-op FPS inspired by extraction shooters and roguelikes. Travel across an alternate-history Japan while building your reputation with various contacts across the country. 

While completing procedurally generated contract missions, you'll encounter a variety of enemies such as organized gangs, corporate strike teams, and foreign PMCs. Each mission has a variety of loot which can be sold or stored for later use, however if you die, you'll lose it as well as the gear you brought in.

Contacts are your main lifeline in this world. They're your shops and fixers. They've also got their own problems. You can help them with those problems. In return, they'll help you source more contracts and help you gain reputation in their region, among other rewards.

As you climb in the underworld, you'll grow stronger with your own unique build with an assortment of stats and associated skill trees. Install various cyberware that enhance your capabilities further, including additional HUD elements. Utilize a variety of weapons and attachments, as well as alternate types of ammo.
