Hi! Thank you to everyone that left feedback and reported issues during the playtest. I've spent most of the last month or so working on fixes and improvements, so I don't have too much new stuff content-wise to show right now. Regardless, I put together a short video showcasing some of the new stuff, mainly the button sounds since that's the easiest to show:

{% include video id="tbZwVeSGR20" provider="youtube" %}

## Improvements
I'll leave most of it as a surprise for the demo version, but here's some brief highlights:
- Button sounds (legitimately don't know how I completely forgot about these lol), some buttons have special sounds (such as accepting quests)
- Added various new minor UI elements: Weapons have little stat bars in their descriptions, there's a little warning that appears once the alert level of a region reaches a certain level, among other things.
- Meta-related effects (time spent multipliers, etc) now have a section in the stats menu that shows the current multipliers for those values.
- Added a post-mission summary screen - This can briefly be seen at the end of the preview video. It's fairly basic at the moment: it only shows the name of the mission, the end result and status of the players, the length of the mission, and each player's kills (or just a general kill count if offline). Planning to expand this in the future, but this is all that's tracked right now.
- Added a tutorial scene (I skipped it for the playtest since I assumed most people playing that would already be familiar with the first game lol, also the shoothouse leaderboard is real this time but times on it won't carry over to the main game)

## Telemetry
Another thing that I probably should've implemented before the playtest but have now is telemetry! With Tokyo, It was initially implemented, but honestly I didn't find it that useful in the form it was presented to me through Unity and mostly forgot about it. I then completely disabled it later after I found out they shut down that part of Unity services. Of course, I'm not using Unity this time, so I've had to build the system myself. As such, I actually *do* have useful data now :)

{% include gallery %}

Still needs work visually of course, and not all of these are missions where I'm actually trying to play the game, but it's pretty fun to see data like this. I also track kills (in a general sense, how many kills are being made with certain weapons and on certain factions), individual deaths (who killed you, what weapon were you using, etc) as well as when quests are completed. I'm really hoping to make some neat infographics with this data, that sounds fun.

## Future
The demo is currently planned to be launched on **December 2nd**. Accompanying it will be another post with everything that's different between the playtest version and the demo. The demo is otherwise ready, so I'm switching back to early access release content now, look out for a more normal dev-blog/preview post in December after the demo's release. That's about it for now, thank you for your support!
